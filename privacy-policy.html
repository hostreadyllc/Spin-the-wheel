Spin the Wheel Privacy Policy

App: Spin the Wheel (com.ash.spinthewheel) · Developer: HostReady LLC · Effective 27 October 2026

In short: Spin the Wheel does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app works without an internet connection.

Information the app stores on your device

To remember your choices between uses, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • The names, numbers or symbols you type onto the wheel
  • How many slices the wheel has
  • Your selected theme
  • Whether sound, vibration and Eliminator mode are on
  • Whether premium themes are unlocked

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send information anywhere. It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party tracking tools.

Device permissions and sensors

  • Vibration is used to make the phone buzz while the wheel spins and when a result is picked. You can turn it off in the app.
  • Motion sensor readings are used only to detect when you shake the phone to spin the wheel. They are processed instantly on the device and are not recorded or stored.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Purchases

If the app offers premium themes for purchase, payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

Spin the Wheel is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
